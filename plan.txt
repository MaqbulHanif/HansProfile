# PROJECT: HANS SUSTAINABLE FARMING — COMPANY PROFILE WEBSITE

## 1. ROLE & OBJECTIVE

Act as a Senior Frontend Developer, UI/UX Designer, and Brand Designer.

Your task is to design and develop a professional, modern, premium, and responsive company profile website for **Hans Sustainable Farming**.

The website represents a family-owned agricultural business operating in:

1. Livestock Farming
2. Aquaculture
3. Plantation
4. Agriculture

The primary objective is to establish a strong brand identity, build business credibility, showcase business activities, and provide contact information for potential business partners and customers.

This is NOT an e-commerce website. Do not prioritize online transactions or shopping cart functionality.

The result should look like a professional agricultural company website, not a generic template or ordinary farming blog.
